\label{app:protocol}

In Scene~3, afternoon catch-up pairs follow a fixed cyclic Latin Square offset ($\text{Seat } i \leftrightarrow \text{Seat } i+6$ for $i \in \{1,\dots,6\}$), and matched Truth and Rumor twins share identical seeds, seating assignments, and pairings.
