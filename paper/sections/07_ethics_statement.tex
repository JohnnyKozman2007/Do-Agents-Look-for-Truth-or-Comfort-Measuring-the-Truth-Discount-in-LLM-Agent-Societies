\section*{Ethical Statement}
\label{sec:ethics_statement}

As LLM agent networks are increasingly used for automated workflows and multi-agent deliberation, understanding when agents pay to verify claims versus rely on source cues or partner reports is essential for designing reliable auditing and verification mechanisms.

To study these dynamics safely without generating blueprints for real-world manipulation, we restricted our experiments to low-stakes community concert announcements with invented artist names and avoided politically sensitive, medical, or financial topics. All experiments consist solely of simulated agent-to-agent interactions; no human subjects were exposed to false claims, and no private personal records were collected or processed.
